Ryokan are traditional Japanese inns that offer a unique cultural experience, featuring tatami rooms, futon bedding, and kaiseki cuisine, making them a must-try for visitors to Japan. They provide more than just accommodation; they offer a chance to experience traditional Japanese hospitality and lifestyle.

They have a very nice car culture

Japanese car culture is a vibrant, multifaceted scene blending street racing, tuning, drifting, and community-driven passion, with a global influence rooted in creativity and performance.Cars in Japan are often seen as extensions of the driver’s personality, combining creativity, craftsmanship, and social identity. The culture merges traditional Japanese ideals of precision and quality with individual expression, making it a unique global phenomenon.
